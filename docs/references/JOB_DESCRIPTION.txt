Position Description
United Network for Organ Sharing (UNOS) is seeking an Associate Data Scientist with a passion for developing data driven products to advance the field of Transplantation. You’ll be part of a team collaborating within the Product and Tech org, working to take AI and machine learning projects from idea, through design and development, into deployment and production. The goal of your work is to have a meaningful impact on the lives of those waiting for transplants or transplant recipients. This role is ideal for recent graduates or early-career professionals with a strong academic foundation in data science, statistics, computer science, or software engineering who are interested in learning how machine learning solutions are developed, deployed, and maintained in production environments.



Key Responsibilities
Support the conversion of analytical prototypes and machine learning models into scalable and maintainable software solutions under the guidance of senior team members.
Develop technical expertise in software development, computer science, and model deployment practices through hands-on project work and mentorship.
Work closely with software development, DevOps, and Data Engineering teams to support model training, testing, and deployment activities.
Integrate, clean, and transform data from diverse structured and unstructured sources, and contribute to the development and maintenance of data pipelines
Apply analytical and data science techniques to derive insights and information from large datasets
Perform analytics on big data assets
Leverage domain knowledge and creativity to generate novel hypotheses 
Explore the adaptation of novel data science and machine learning applications to current issues in Transplantation

Minimum Requirements
Experience in data science, analytics, computer programming, or related fields through graduate research, internships, academic projects, assistantships, or professional employment.
Coursework, research, project experience, or exposure to high-performance computing or big data tools (e.g., Spark, Hive) preferred.


Critical Skills
Ability to assimilate and disseminate new software tools and programming techniques
Strong demonstrated desire to learn new skills and techniques
Strong written and oral English-language communication skills
The ability to meet deadlines, and to work independently and as part of a team
Exposure to cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud through coursework, internships, projects, or professional experience
Familiarity with Git and version control workflows
Exposure to software testing concepts and CI/CD practices
Familiarity with APIs and how applications exchange data
Rapid prototyping, hackathon, and/or front-end project experience 
 
Additional Skills & Qualifications
Coursework, academic projects, or practical experience using R, SQL, and/or Python
Experience with large (terabytes) databases
Familiarity with software development lifecycle concepts
Exposure to working within terminal emulators 
Experience using AI-assisted software development tools (e.g., Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or similar)
Interest in emerging AI technologies, including agentic systems, AI-assisted development, and LLM-enabled applications

Education
Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Bioinformatics, Applied Mathematics, or a related field strongly preferred. Recent graduates are encouraged to apply.
