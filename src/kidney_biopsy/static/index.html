Input requirements and model details +

The CSV starts with a specimen ID column, followed by all required raw assay targets, including the 12 housekeeping targets. Target columns may be reordered. Missing, duplicate, extra, nonnumeric, negative, or non-finite measurements fail the entire batch. Specimen IDs must be present and unique.

The caller must establish that measurements came from the compatible assay. A valid numeric file alone cannot establish assay compatibility.
